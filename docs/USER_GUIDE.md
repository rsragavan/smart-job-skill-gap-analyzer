# User Guide

## Getting started

1. Open the application URL.
2. Create an account (**Register**) or **Login**.
3. Use the sidebar (desktop) or navbar menu (mobile).

## Upload your resume

1. Go to **Upload Resume**.
2. Select a PDF/DOCX file within the size limit.
3. After upload, review extracted skills and recommended jobs.
4. You can set a recommended job as your target and start learning.

## Choose a target (required for roadmap)

Open **Target** (route `/jobs`).

### Option A — Scraped company

1. Search/filter Greenhouse-synced jobs (GitLab, Cloudflare, etc.).
2. Click select on a job card.
3. The app matches your resume, saves the active target, generates a roadmap, and opens **Roadmap**.

### Option B — Custom company

1. Switch to **Custom Company**.
2. Enter company name, role, and paste the full job description.
3. Click **Generate Analysis & Roadmap**.
4. Works for companies not in the database (Zoho, TCS, startups, etc.).

Clear the active target anytime from the banner on the Target page.

## Roadmap

View missing skills with topics, resources, projects, milestones, XP, and estimated days. Source badge shows Scraped vs Custom.

## Learning

Open **Learning** to:

- Mark topics in progress / completed  
- Complete practice missions  
- Finish projects in order (later projects unlock after earlier ones)  
- Earn XP, levels, badges  
- Track daily and weekly goals  

## Career GPS

Shows readiness scores, current match %, remaining skills, market demand, recommendations, and timeline. Prefers your active target company and role.

## Analytics

Charts and stats for resume, jobs, learning, roadmap, career, and XP. Filters default from your active target when set.

## Applications & Dashboard

Track applications for scraped jobs from job cards. Dashboard summarizes application statuses and highlights your active target.

## Profile

View account details and log out. Toggle dark mode from the theme control in the layout/navbar.

## Tips

- Always upload a resume before selecting a target.  
- Custom JD quality directly affects skill extraction quality.  
- Progress is saved per roadmap; changing targets creates a new roadmap plan.
